# Make a video

1. Clone [the studio repository](https://github.com/maan-iitd2/designer-video-studio) or extract its ZIP into its own folder. Open **designer-video-studio** as the project in **Claude Code** on your computer.
2. Type your idea. For example, **“I want a reel about my lettering project. I have sketches and a screen recording. Help me choose a look.”** If you prefer a command, type `/direct-video` followed by your idea.
3. Pick a visual direction. You can reply with a number, a link, a screenshot, a timestamp, or “choose for me.” English and Hinglish both work.

Claude will ask whether you have a script or want help writing one. It will show you a short sample using your material before making the whole video. You can say **“keep the movement from the first one, use the colours from the second”** or **“change only the ending.”**

You can start with just an idea. If you already have photos, footage, a voice note or a brand kit, drop them in the project and tell Claude which files to use. For a private Pinterest board, one or two screenshots are enough.

**See two starting looks:** [Paper collage, 8 seconds](starters/paper-film-starter/demo.mp4) · [Kinetic type, 4 seconds](starters/kinetic-type/pilot.mp4).

## Browse the shot library

Open [the online gallery](https://maan-iitd2.github.io/designer-video-studio/) on a phone or computer. Each shot links to its editable source and the original tool.

In Windows Explorer, right-click the ZIP and choose **Extract All**. In the extracted **designer-video-studio** folder, double-click **Open Shot Library.html**. The gallery opens in your browser. Search for a subject or move, open a card and copy its ID. Try “Use this shot for the middle of my reel” with the ID. The catalog, source files, stills and bundled MP4s open locally. Individual templates may use web fonts, media or renderer packages.

## Cost and setup

You can give Claude a budget if you have one. It favours reusable assets and local rendering, and quotes paid media or generation before using it.

Style selection works immediately. Claude sets up the renderer for the style you choose. Export runs on your computer. The bundled paper starter uses Python, Playwright and FFmpeg. The kinetic starter uses Node and HyperFrames. Other styles have their own tools.

## What you keep

Each film gets its own folder with your brief, approved shots, asset credits, editable project and finished MP4. Keep that folder for revisions. For a second film, say **“use my last video's colours and titles”** and Claude can reuse those decisions.
