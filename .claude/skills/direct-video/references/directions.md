# Choose the look

Pick the closest two ideas for this brief. Link the specific example. Give the designer a concrete distinction she can see. She may mix references or bring her own.

| If she wants… | Show her… | The decision to draw out |
|---|---|---|
| An editorial or Vox-like explainer | The eight-second `starters/paper-film-starter/demo.mp4` and the [engraving-style ad](https://prompt-motion.com/lexnlin-038035) | Should the words feel printed on a page, or should ink marks move across her photos? |
| A word-led title or showreel | The four-second `starters/kinetic-type/pilot.mp4` and the [kinetic showreel](https://prompt-motion.com/templates/showreel) | Which word should land hardest? Should the next scene grow out of that word? |
| A product or portfolio walkthrough | [Photo print app launch film](https://prompt-motion.com/twoclipping-221cab) and the [Shotcraft gallery](https://vincentwei1021.github.io/video-shotcraft/) | Follow one interaction, or reveal a sequence of project cards? Ask for her screen recording or screens. |
| An illustrated or diagram-led explanation | [Animated business explainer](https://prompt-motion.com/alex-prompter-1ea044) | What relationship, change or comparison does the viewer need to understand? Request the real steps or figures. |
| A spoken reel or documentary cut | [Talking-head edit](https://prompt-motion.com/sab8a-364a5a) and the [finished Herk edits](https://github.com/nateherkai/herk-edit-kit#examples) | Stay with the speaker, or cut away to evidence as a key line lands? |
| A photographic or cinematic montage | The photo-grid sequence in the [print film](https://prompt-motion.com/twoclipping-221cab), paired with her closest film reference | Is the appeal framing, movement, lighting, or the rhythm of the cuts? Use her photos or footage as the first test. |
| Character work, 3D, mixed media or another direction | Start with her reference. For 3D, show the [3D template in the Prompt Motion collection](https://prompt-motion.com/templates). For another style, find a specific relevant example. | Can the effect be achieved with layered stills, existing animation or one short generated insert? Price the expensive part separately. |

The gallery offers copyable prompts and some entries link to skills. Examples are credited to their creators. Copy only the useful move, timing or technique into the brief. Use assets she owns or can license in the exported film. A Pinterest pin can guide composition without becoming a production asset. Record its original source if sourcing a usable copy.
